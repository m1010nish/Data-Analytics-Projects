# Data Analyst Intern Assignment – Zylentrix Learning Platform

This project is a solution to a real-world data analytics case study for the position of **Data Analyst Intern**. It involves analyzing user engagement on an online learning platform and deriving actionable business insights.

---

## 📌 Project Objective

To analyze student behavior, course engagement, and feedback patterns from an online training platform and provide strategic recommendations for improving user experience and course effectiveness.

---

## 📂 Dataset Overview

The analysis was performed using three CSV files:

- `students.csv`: Student ID, Name, Age, Gender, Location, Enrollment Date  
- `course_activity.csv`: Course ID, Student ID, Time Spent, Completion %  
- `feedback.csv`: Course ID, Student ID, Rating (1-5), Feedback Text  

---

## 🧹 Key Steps in the Analysis

1. **Data Cleaning & Preparation**
   - Handled missing and duplicate data
   - Converted and standardized date/time formats
   - Merged datasets for unified analysis

2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**
   - Analyzed engagement patterns by location, age group, and gender
   - Identified top-rated and most engaging courses
   - Compared course performance and student behavior

3. **Visualizations**
   - Bar plots of time spent by location and course
   - Gender-wise monthly engagement trends
   - Student segmentation chart

4. **Segmentation**
   - Grouped students into High, Medium, and Low performers
   - Based on rating and time spent

5. **Insights & Recommendations**
   - 5 key insights about student engagement and course performance
   - 3 data-driven business recommendations to enhance platform usage

---

## 📊 Key Insights

- `DM101` is the most engaging course in terms of time spent
- `PY202` has the highest average feedback rating
- Delhi, Bangalore, and Chennai show the most active viewership
- Gender-based viewing patterns vary by month
- Age group 24–28 is the most engaged and responsive

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

1. Promote top-performing courses (`PY202` and `DM101`) to improve enrollments.
2. Launch city-specific campaigns in high-engagement areas like Delhi, Bangalore, and Chennai.
3. Target engagement strategies by gender-month trends (e.g., female-focused events in January).
